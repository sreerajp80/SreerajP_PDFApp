# Privacy Policy — Folio

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026
**App:** Folio (`in.sreerajp.pdfapp`), an offline PDF app for Android. Shown as ഗ്രന്ഥം in
Malayalam and ग्रन्थः in Sanskrit.
**Contact:** sreerajp@zohomail.in

This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **the app collects
nothing, sends nothing to any server, and your PDFs stay on your phone.**

## 1. What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting
and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no data from the app, ever. The app never
contacts any internet server.

## 2. How the app opens your files

The app can open only the files you give it:

- files you pick in Android's own file picker, and
- files you open with **Open with** or **Share** from another app.

It does not ask for permission to read all your storage, and it has no file browser of its own.
For files in your **Recent files** list, it keeps Android's permission to open that one file
again, until you remove it from the list.

## 3. What stays on your phone

To work, the app keeps some information **on your phone only**, in the app's private storage:

- **Recent files:** file name, page count, a small picture of the first page and the date you
  last opened it (you can turn this off or clear it in Settings).
- **What you add:** reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes and drawings, and your
  settings. Your marks are kept apart from the PDF; the PDF itself is never changed.
- **Trusted certificates** that you import to check digital signatures.
- **Temporary copies** of open or printed files, removed by the app's cache cleanup.

**Passwords** you type to open a protected PDF are kept in memory only while needed, and are
never saved or logged.

Every page tool (merge, split, rotate, delete, compress, protect, unlock and so on) writes a
**new file** where you choose. Your original file is never changed.

None of this is copied to cloud backup or to another phone by Android's backup or device-transfer
features. **Settings → Storage & Privacy → Delete all data** removes all of it; it never deletes
your own PDFs.

## 4. Device sync (phone to phone)

Device sync can copy your app settings, or PDFs you choose, to **your other phone**. It is the
only time the app uses the network:

- **Settings by QR code only:** the settings are inside the QR code. No network is used.
- **Over Wi-Fi:** the phones talk directly over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot, using private, local
  network addresses only — never the internet, and never through any server.
- Pairing is done by scanning a QR code. Every message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a
  one-time key that exists only in that QR code and in memory.
- The connection accepts one phone, and stops after 3 minutes, when you leave the screen, or
  when the app goes to the background.
- The receiving phone shows what will arrive and saves nothing until you tap **Apply** or
  **Accept**.
- Your recent files list, reading positions, marks, bookmarks and certificates are never sent.

## 5. Read aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone (for example, Google's
speech services). The app gives the page text to that engine on your phone. What that engine
does is governed by its maker's own privacy policy.

## 6. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | Only to scan the Device sync QR code. Asked the first time you open the scan screen. The app never takes or saves photos |
| Internet, network state | Only for Device sync on your own Wi-Fi. Android needs the "internet" permission for any network connection, even a local one |

The app never records audio and has no permission to read all your storage.

## 7. Sharing and printing

The app shares or prints a file only when you tap **Share** or **Print** and choose an app or
printer. What that app or printer service then does is governed by its own privacy policy.

## 8. Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## 9. Deleting your information

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Use **Delete all data** in Settings, or uninstall the
app, to remove it. Files you saved with the app (for example, a merged PDF) are your own files
and stay where you saved them.
